Open Road

Walk the city, climb the hills, cross the desert. Steal any car and drive it.

Keyboard: WASD move or drive, Shift run, Space jump or handbrake, E get in or out, M big map. Hold the right mouse button and drag to look. Left click punches or fires toward your cursor. Keys 1, 2, 3 or Q switch fists, pistol and rifle. Walk over glowing guns to pick them up. E also enters buildings and eats or drinks at food stands and inside homes. Hurting civilians brings the police. Watch out for gangs in red and black. Esc opens the pause menu, P your phone, C crouches, V changes the vehicle camera, N toggles the engine. Sleep in a property you own to save.

Phone: left stick moves, drag the right side to look, buttons on the right.
